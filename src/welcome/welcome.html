Your browser installed Breakless with some sites switched off (Microsoft Edge does this). It can't skip ads on a site it isn't allowed on.

Good to know

  • Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar and pin Breakless to see what it's doing. Reload any video tab that was already open.
  • YouTube: Breakless removes ads before they load; any that still start are skipped (Skip is clicked the moment it appears, or the ad is muted and jumped to its end). Ads in the feed, search and sidebar are hidden. If a video won't play, switch "Remove YouTube ads before they load" off in the Breakless menu.
  • Prime Video ads are removed before they load. If subtitles drift or a title won't play, switch "Remove Prime ads before they load" off in the Breakless menu.
  • Live sports ads can't be skipped: Breakless mutes them until the match is back.
