Breakless is installed

Movies, shows and videos without ad breaks. Two quick checks and you're set.

1

Let Breakless run on your streaming sites

Some browsers, Microsoft Edge among them, install extensions with site access switched off. Breakless can't skip ads on a site it isn't allowed on.

  • Prime Video
  • JioHotstar

All set. Breakless can work on Prime Video and JioHotstar.

2

YouTube New

Skips YouTube video ads (clicks Skip the moment it's allowed, or mutes the ad and jumps to its end) and hides ads in the feed, sidebar and search. It's optional: Breakless only runs on YouTube after you switch it on here.

YouTube is on. Reload any YouTube tab that's already open.

Good to know

  • Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar and pin Breakless to see what it's doing.
  • Prime Video ads are now removed before they load, on by default. If subtitles drift or a title won't play, switch "Remove Prime ads before they load" off in the Breakless menu.
  • Live sports ads can't be skipped: Breakless mutes them until the match is back.
