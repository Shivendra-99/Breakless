# Breakless privacy policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Breakless is a browser extension that skips ads in movies, shows and videos on supported video websites.

## What Breakless collects

Nothing. Breakless does not collect, store, sell or send any personal information, browsing history, viewing history or usage data. It has no analytics, no tracking and no servers.

## What stays on your device

Breakless saves a few settings in your browser's local extension storage:

- which sites it is switched on for
- which optional modes are on
- a counter of ad breaks it has handled

This data never leaves your browser and is deleted when you remove the extension.

## Website access

Breakless only runs on the websites it supports: Prime Video and JioHotstar pages, Prime Video pages on Amazon domains, and youtube.com if you switch YouTube on and allow it. There it reads the video player to detect ad breaks, hides ad slots and blocks known ad requests. It does not read or change anything else. You can switch YouTube off, or remove its access, at any time.
